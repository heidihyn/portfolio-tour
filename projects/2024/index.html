Good to know

Tips from the clubhouse

i.

Tee times are firm

The course and the Bungalow are reserved for us, and tee times are not approximate. Please arrive by 3:30pm to check in.

ii.

Check in ahead of time

Draw your team and pick your strengths on this page before the day, so you can head straight to the first tee.

iii.

Teams mix departments

Your putt decides your team, so expect to play alongside people you don't usually work with.

iv.

Save room for supper

The food trucks run from 4:45pm to 5:45pm, right after your round.

v.

Find the Bungalow

It's ours from 5:30pm, and trivia starts there at 5:45pm.

vi.

Dress for a course

Smart casual is the guide. You'll be on your feet for a round, so wear comfortable shoes.
