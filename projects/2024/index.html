Good to know

Tips from the clubhouse

i.

Be on the green on time

The course and the Bungalow are reserved for us, and tee times won't hold. Aim for 5:45pm or 6:00pm sharp.

ii.

Build your trivia team early

Teams are friend groups. Invite the people you'd want beside you, and pick your categories below so we know your strengths.

iii.

Save room for supper

The food trucks run from about 6:30pm to 7:30pm, right after your round.

iv.

Find the Bungalow

It's ours from 7:00pm, and trivia starts there at 7:30pm.

v.

Dress for a course

Dressy casual is the guide. Whatever you wear, you'll be on your feet for a round of golf.

vi.

Scout the course
